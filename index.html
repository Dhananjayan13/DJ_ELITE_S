Our Story

DJ Elite Sarees

Born in the weaving heartland of Salem, DJ Elite Sarees is a tribute to India's timeless drape. For decades, our family has worked alongside master weavers to bring you sarees that carry both tradition and grace.

From hand-woven Ellampillai silks to delicate Banarasi weaves and breathable Salem cottons — each saree in our collection is selected with love, an eye for craft, and a commitment to authenticity.

Visit us at our flagship store in Ammapet, Salem and experience the heritage in person.
